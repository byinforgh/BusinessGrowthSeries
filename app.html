Welcome to the family

We're genuinely excited you're here. This is a community of Ghanaian business owners, and people just starting out, learning to price better, sell more, and grow with people who get it. Here's how to get the most out of it.

Start here

  • Open the Curriculum tab and go through Lesson 1, it sets up everything after it.
  • Say hello in the Community feed, one line is plenty.
  • Check the Business Hour bubble (top right) for the next live session, and join it.
  • Add yourself to the Directory properly, that's how other members and real customers find you.

What you get here

  • Weekly live Business Hour sessions you can ask questions in.
  • A structured curriculum you can go at your own pace.
  • A Q&A space where members answer members.
  • A directory and connections tab to find customers, suppliers, and collaborators.
  • A wins feed, post them, however small, people here will celebrate them with you.

Do

  • Show up as yourself, real questions and real wins beat a polished front.
  • Give as much as you take, answer a question if you know the answer.
  • Keep your profile and directory listing up to date, it's how people reach you.

Don't

  • Don't post unrelated ads or spam in Community or Q&A.
  • Don't share another member's contact info without asking them first.
  • Don't be discouraged if growth is slow, that's normal, keep showing up.
