My data

See what we hold, download a copy, choose what other members see, or delete your account. You do not need to ask us for permission or wait for a reply.

Download my data

You get one file with your profile, progress, posts, questions, Arena plays, messages you sent, notifications and activity records. Private details such as your email and struggle are included because they are yours.

My phone number

Optional. If you add a number, other members can see it in the directory so customers can reach you on WhatsApp. Remove it any time and it disappears from the directory straight away.

My choices

Show me on the Arena weekly boardWhen on, your name and business name can appear on the Business Arena board for other members.
Email remindersWeekly Business Hour and progress reminders by email. Turn off to stop them. Login emails still arrive, because you need them to sign in.

Delete my account

This permanently deletes:

  • your profile, progress, check ins, Arena plays and badges
  • your posts, questions, answers, wins, reviews and suggestions
  • every direct, group and Live Lounge message you sent
  • your logo and spotlight photos
  • your connections, notifications and activity records

It cannot be undone. Business Hour attendance records are deleted with the account. If your account was suspended for breaking the rules, contact us on WhatsApp instead, so we can handle it properly.
