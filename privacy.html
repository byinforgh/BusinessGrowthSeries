Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Business Growth Series

Plain language, Act 843 in view: this policy is written to be genuinely understandable and to reflect the requirements of Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) as they apply to BYINFORGH. It is prepared for a Ghanaian lawyer's expedited sign-off, not a substitute for it. A lawyer should still confirm registration status and review the wording before wide promotion. See the Data Controller Registration section below for where things currently stand.

Data controller registration (Act 843, Section 27(1))

Under Section 27(1) of the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), every organisation that processes personal data in Ghana must register as a data controller with the Data Protection Commission (DPC) before processing begins, and renew that registration every two years. BYINFORGH is the data controller for everything collected on this platform.

Status: registration with the Data Protection Commission is a required next step before this platform is promoted beyond a small pilot group, and is being progressed alongside legal review. Processing personal data without registering is itself an offence under the Act, so this is treated as a priority action, not an afterthought.

The eight data protection principles we follow (Act 843, Section 17)

  • Accountability: BYINFORGH is responsible for how member data is handled, and that responsibility isn't outsourced to Supabase or any other provider we use.
  • Lawfulness of processing: data is only processed with a member's knowledge, through the signup and profile forms they fill in themselves.
  • Specification of purpose: each piece of data is collected for a stated reason, listed in "Why we collect it" below, not for undefined future use.
  • Compatibility of further processing: we don't repurpose data for something a member wouldn't reasonably expect from a business growth platform.
  • Quality of information: members can correct their own profile at any time, and can ask BYINFORGH to fix anything they can't edit themselves.
  • Openness: this page states plainly what's collected, why, and who can see it, matching the notice requirements in Section 35.
  • Data security safeguards: enforced at the database level through Row Level Security, not just hidden in the interface. See "Where your data lives" below.
  • Data subject participation: members can access, correct, or request deletion of their own data at any time. See "Your rights" below.

What we collect

When you join, we collect your name, WhatsApp or phone number, email address, business name, category, location and nearest landmark, what you sell, what you're looking for, and your biggest current struggle. If you choose to add one, we also store a business logo or photo you upload. We automatically record when you were last active, so the community can show who's online.

Why we collect it

  • To create your business profile so other members and the AI assistant can actually help you
  • To show you in the business directory so other members can find, trade, or partner with you
  • To calculate your accountability streak and the community leaderboard
  • To send you your login link by email
  • To improve the AI assistant, only using documents and questions you or other members explicitly submit and BYINFORGH reviews and approves, never your private profile fields

Who can see what

Your name, business name, category, location, landmark, what you sell, what you're looking for, phone number, and logo are visible to other logged in members, since that visibility is the point of the directory and community. Your email address and what you told us about your biggest struggle are private, visible only to you and to BYINFORGH for support purposes, never shown to other members.

Content you post in the community feed, the Q&A hub, or submit as a business spotlight is visible to other logged in members, since those are public spaces by design. Nothing you post there is private.

Where your data lives

Data is stored with Supabase, a database and authentication provider, inside a project controlled by BYINFORGH. Photos and logos are stored the same way, in a dedicated storage bucket. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Because Supabase's infrastructure may process or store data outside Ghana, this is a cross-border transfer under Act 843. BYINFORGH only uses providers that maintain their own data security standards, and this arrangement is one of the specific items being confirmed as part of the ongoing legal review.

Your rights

You can ask to see everything we hold about you, correct anything that's wrong, withdraw consent, object to how your data is used, or have your account and data deleted entirely. To do any of this, message BYINFORGH directly on WhatsApp or email, using the contact details on the main site. In line with Section 35 of Act 843, we aim to respond to any request within 40 days at the very most, and usually much sooner. Deletion removes your profile, posts, and photos from the platform.

If something goes wrong (breach notification)

If member data is ever compromised in a way that puts you at risk, BYINFORGH will notify the Data Protection Commission as soon as possible, and will notify affected members directly, explaining what happened and what we're doing about it. We don't wait for a complaint to act on a known security issue.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, since that's what makes the directory, community, and progress tracking work. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile, posts, and photos within a reasonable time, except where we're required to keep limited records for legal or security reasons.

Children

This platform is built for business owners and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll make a reasonable effort to let members know, for example through the community feed or a direct message.

Terms of use, in short

  • Everything you post should be honest and about your actual business, no fake profiles or fake reviews
  • Be respectful in the community, no harassment, hate speech, or scam attempts against other members
  • Content you submit to train the AI assistant is reviewed by BYINFORGH before it's used, and should be genuinely yours to share
  • BYINFORGH can remove content or suspend accounts that break these terms
  • The 203 lessons, playbooks, and resources are for your own business use, not for resale
  • Lessons, playbooks, and guides are read inside the platform and aren't provided as downloadable files, so the community, questions, and shared experience around them stay part of the same space

Contact

For any privacy question, data request, or concern, reach BYINFORGH directly through the contact details on the main site.
