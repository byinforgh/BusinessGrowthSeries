Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Business Growth Series

Plain language notice: this policy is written to be genuinely understandable, not just legally defensible. It is a starting point, not a substitute for a Ghanaian lawyer's review. Before this goes fully live, especially given the volume of personal data this collects, get it checked against Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), including whether BYINFORGH needs to register as a data controller with the Data Protection Commission.

What we collect

When you join, we collect your name, WhatsApp or phone number, email address, business name, category, location and nearest landmark, what you sell, what you're looking for, and your biggest current struggle. If you choose to add one, we also store a business logo or photo you upload. We automatically record when you were last active, so the community can show who's online.

Why we collect it

  • To create your business profile so other members and the AI assistant can actually help you
  • To show you in the business directory so other members can find, trade, or partner with you
  • To calculate your accountability streak and the community leaderboard
  • To send you your login link by email
  • To improve the AI assistant, only using documents and questions you or other members explicitly submit and BYINFORGH reviews and approves, never your private profile fields

Who can see what

Your name, business name, category, location, landmark, what you sell, what you're looking for, phone number, and logo are visible to other logged in members, since that visibility is the point of the directory and community. Your email address and what you told us about your biggest struggle are private, visible only to you and to BYINFORGH for support purposes, never shown to other members.

Content you post in the community feed, the Q&A hub, or submit as a business spotlight is visible to other logged in members, since those are public spaces by design. Nothing you post there is private.

Where your data lives

Data is stored with Supabase, a database and authentication provider, inside a project controlled by BYINFORGH. Photos and logos are stored the same way, in a dedicated storage bucket. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Your rights

You can ask to see everything we hold about you, correct anything that's wrong, or have your account and data deleted entirely. To do any of this, message BYINFORGH directly on WhatsApp or email, using the contact details on the main site. We aim to act on any request within a reasonable time, and deletion removes your profile, posts, and photos from the platform.

Children

This platform is built for business owners and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll make a reasonable effort to let members know, for example through the community feed or a direct message.

Terms of use, in short

  • Everything you post should be honest and about your actual business, no fake profiles or fake reviews
  • Be respectful in the community, no harassment, hate speech, or scam attempts against other members
  • Content you submit to train the AI assistant is reviewed by BYINFORGH before it's used, and should be genuinely yours to share
  • BYINFORGH can remove content or suspend accounts that break these terms
  • The 202 lessons, playbooks, and resources are for your own business use, not for resale

Contact

For any privacy question, data request, or concern, reach BYINFORGH directly through the contact details on the main site.
