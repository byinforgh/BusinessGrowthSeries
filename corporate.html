The customer journey we built

A guest scans the QR code, picks a location and table, browses the digital menu, enters a name and phone number and pays by cash or MoMo. The order lands on the right dashboard, staff prepare and deliver it, and the guest sees a membership profile with points.

  • Combo orders split automatically between canteen and bar.
  • Three stock points tracked separately, with transfers counted once.
  • Cash and MoMo on separate tabs, payment confirmed before an order is marked paid.
  • Every sale tied to a staff name and a customer phone or member ID.

Built live, tested hard, hardened

  • Pilot: 30 fixes closed, from price corrections to staff login and returning guests not recognised.
  • Re-platform: the first build hit busy errors at three or more simultaneous orders, so the back end moved to Supabase while the front end kept its look.
  • Security audit: all 11 original dashboard files reviewed, issues patched, the members dashboard gated and destructive actions tied to a verified PIN.
  • Governance: refund and escalation matrix, crisis response, health and safety guide, each with a verification path.

People, standards and a content engine

  • Training: one title, Hospitality Specialist, across stations. 25 modules in five groups, scene based and written in plain English with local values.
  • Accountability: the Golden Rule (Clean, Check, Restock, Report, Reset), eight owned zones, a Green, Amber, Red gap system and three escalation tiers.
  • Content: analysis of 208 posts showed 36 to 50 second videos averaged 1,562 views against 516 for videos under 20 seconds. The rebuild replaced 184 captions with 85 unique captions and 18 video formats.
  • Programmes for quiet nights: Kof-City Games League and Winner Stays On pool league, with a shared leaderboard.
